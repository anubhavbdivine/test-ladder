How XP and the two-day rule work
  • Each completed task earns 10 XP, capped at 100 task XP per day.
  • Complete at least 60% of today's tasks to secure the day: +30 XP. At least three tasks are needed unless your list contains fewer than three.
  • Complete the entire list for another +20 XP.
  • One missed day is allowed.
  • Two consecutive missed days reset current run XP and run days. Lifetime XP, best run, and workout history remain.
  • A day ends at local midnight. Today is never marked missed before it ends.
  • Unticking tasks removes their XP and any bonuses no longer earned. Repeated ticking does not generate extra XP.
  • Run days count secured days, not grace days. A secured day restores your allowance for one consecutive missed day.

This is a personal accountability tool, not a competition. Rest and recovery can be legitimate checklist tasks.
